### Contracts (`contracts/`, Foundry, solc 0.8.26, forge 1.5.1)

- `KeyfleetPresets.sol`: the four presets' arithmetic (MAX_Q 1e24; Fibonacci 20
  levels, supplies 2, 3, 5, 8, …, max token id 46,365, price ceilDiv(prev×120,
  100) per level, holding limit 1 through level 7 then supply/10; Classic 144 at
  q; Accelerant ceilDiv(prev×105, 100) per primary mint, cap 2000; bps 500
  protocol / 1000 referral / 1000 Accelerant capital).
- `KeyfleetRewards.sol`: per-fleet reward ledger (PRECISION 1e27, 8 assets, 32
  cohorts, backing checked, settle-before-transfer, fractional carry). The fleet
  must enroll every key at mint.
- `Keyfleet.sol`: the ERC-721 fleet. Deploys its own Rewards and Votes in the
  constructor. Constructor order: preset, q, regent, usdc, treasury,
  assetRegistry, staking, factory, votingWindow, Metadata{name, symbol,
  baseTokenURI} (the struct exists because of stack depth). Purchases, ownership
  epochs (`OwnershipEpoch` event, mint = epoch 1), holding limits, free capital
  per asset, Cascade reserve, `depositCapital`, `depositRevenue`,
  `recognizeSurplus` (2% fee: USDC via `RegentRevenueStaking.depositUSDC`, other
  assets to the treasury), `executeAutolaunch` (only Votes; calls the pinned factory with itself as
  treasury, verifies provenance, one launch per fleet), `syncLaunch`,
  `launchSettled()`. No admin, no pause, no upgrade. USDC is pinned to the
  registry's `BASE_USDC`. Free capital is the budget. Once the launch has
  graduated or failed, `recognizeSurplus` (which syncs a settled-but-unsynced
  launch first) splits the post-fee amount: `BUDGET_BPS` 2000 to free capital,
  the rest to the keys through the ledger by the same cohort rule as
  `depositRevenue` (`LaunchIncomeSplit` with the caller; gross = fee + toBudget
  + toKeys). Before that it books everything as free capital
  (`SurplusRecognized`), unchanged. The budget leaves only through
  `payFromBudget` (only Votes, only settled), capped at `PAY_CAP_BPS` 2000 of the
  asset's free capital measured at the first payment of each `PAY_PERIOD` (30
  days, starting at that payment; per period, not rolling, so up to about 36%
  across a boundary); over the cap reverts `PayCapExceeded` at execution
  (`BudgetPaid`, `payPeriod(asset)`). The Safe admits at most 7 assets besides
  USDC per fleet (ledger limit 8), and a regular release-and-recognize job is
  disclosed in the README as Regent's (the job is a later platform slice).
- `KeyfleetVotes.sol`: one fixed vote. Kinds: Decision (0), Autolaunch (1) and
  Pay (2). One vote per key on every kind and preset; `yesVotes`/`noVotes` are
  key counts. Eligible set snapshotted at creation, each key votes once,
  delegate per owner, window bounded 1 hour to 30 days, pass = yes×2 >
  snapshotMinted. `proposePay(tokenId, asset, amount, recipient, text, digest)`
  is refused with `LaunchNotSettled` before the launch settles and with
  `RecipientNotAllowed` for zero, the fleet, Votes or the ledger; the text goes in
  `ProposalCreated` only, and `PayProposed` carries `executionExpiry` =
  closesAt + `PAY_EXECUTION_WINDOW` (30 days). A passed Pay waits for room under
  the cap until then and is refused with `ProposalExpired` from the second after.
  An Autolaunch can be proposed only once
  the fleet has sold 86 keys (Fibonacci: the last key of level 7) or 100 keys
  (Classic, Accelerant), else `LaunchNotYetEligible(minted, required)`; its
  envelope is checked against the factory's and strategy's live limits at
  proposal time.
- `KeyfleetAssetRegistry.sol`: Safe-only global and per-fleet token flags.
- `interfaces/IRegentsAutolaunchFactoryV1.sol`, `IRegentLBPStrategy.sol`:
  minimal copies checked selector-for-selector against the frozen ABI.
- `docs/autolaunch-target.md` is the binding description of the real target
  (`RegentsAutolaunchFactoryV1` in the Autolaunch repo, worktree
  `worktrees/autolaunch/robinhood-stocks`; predicted Base address
  `0x635615cCEF2Ef24D0655fC2eBC47a14e005FEF6e`, a prediction until the ceremony
  manifest names it).

### Platform (`platform/`, Phoenix 1.8, Ash 3.33, AshPostgres)

- Domains: `KeyFleet.Fleets` (fixed catalog of four), `KeyFleet.Agents`
  (wallet-owned profiles, `public_profile` read = id, name, harness, status),
  `KeyFleet.Keys` (`KeyAttachment` = immutable ownership epochs with a

  canonical path spelling.
- LiveView: `ShellLive` hosts the app pages; `KeysLive` at
  `/app/keys/:fleet_id/:token_id` shows owner, operator, requests and history in
  customer language.
- Test isolation quirk: `key_attachments_test` commits the fleet catalog and
  epoch rows for the wallets of all `a`s and all `b`s outside the sandbox on
  purpose (epochs cannot be deleted). Never assume an empty `fleets` table and
  never use those two wallets as "neutral" actors.
