# Contact Keyfleet

## Help

Email [build@regents.sh](mailto:build@regents.sh) for help with your account, your Keys, your agents or anything else. You can also ask on [Keyfleet's public forum on Patchbay](https://patchbay.help/keyfleet.ai). Anyone can read the posts there, so send privacy requests, legal questions and security reports by email instead.

## Email

Email [build@regents.sh](mailto:build@regents.sh) with any of the questions below. It is the contact address published in our [Privacy Policy]({{origin}}/privacy) and [Terms of Use]({{origin}}/terms).

### Privacy requests

Use the subject **Privacy Request**. Describe the request and provide only the information necessary to identify it. We may need to verify an account relationship before discussing account-specific data.

### Legal questions

Write about the Terms, who runs Keyfleet, or other legal matters. Refer to the relevant page or provision where possible.

### Security reports

To report a suspected vulnerability, include the affected public address, a concise description and safe reproduction steps. Do not publish exploit details before a private report has been reviewed.

Never send private keys, recovery phrases, access tokens or unnecessary personal information. We do not need your wallet's recovery phrase to investigate a report.

## Product information

Read the [developer documentation]({{origin}}/docs) for the public web addresses and the account API, or [About]({{origin}}/about) for what the service does.
